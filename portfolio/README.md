# Peng Zhuo | Data and Business Analytics Portfolio

Statistics undergraduate, expected to graduate in July 2027. I frame business questions, define measurable indicators, use SQL, Python, and statistical methods to examine evidence, and translate findings into decision-ready recommendations.

**[View the 16-page portfolio (PDF)](./PZ_Data_Business_Analytics_Portfolio_EN.pdf)**

## Projects

1. **E-commerce fulfillment and customer experience** — Analyzed public Olist order data to prioritize delivery-stage and geographic investigations. My contribution: metric design, SQL implementation, and result validation.
2. **Digital transformation of a Luzhou seafood market** — Combined 402 valid consumer survey responses with July 2025 sales records to prioritize transaction-transparency pilots and customer segments. My contribution: survey design, field research, and core data analysis.
3. **Aging and health-system resilience in Chongqing** — Built a four-dimension resilience proxy from 38 districts and counties over 2020-2024, then examined conditional associations using panel models. My contribution: data collection, analysis, model development, and conclusion writing.

The portfolio distinguishes observed findings from proposed actions. Recommendations shown in it have not been presented as implemented outcomes. The health-system findings are conditional associations, not causal estimates.
